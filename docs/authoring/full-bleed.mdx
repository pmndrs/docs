<Intro>
  A block as wide as the article, past the reading column.
</Intro>

Every block of a page is no wider than the reading column. With `full-bleed` in its `className`, a block takes the whole width of the article instead, from the `xl` breakpoint, without the side margin other blocks keep: it reaches the article's edges. The difference only shows on a screen wide enough for the article to be wider than the column.

## Usage

On a block at the top level of the page, not nested in another one:

```jsx
<div className="full-bleed flex h-40 items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">full-bleed</div>
```

result:

<div className="full-bleed flex h-40 items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">full-bleed</div>

compared to a block without it:

<div className="flex h-40 items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">default</div>
