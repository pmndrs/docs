<Intro>
  A block as wide as the article, past the reading column, as `Sandpack` is.
</Intro>

Every block of a page is no wider than the reading column. With `full-bleed` in its `className`, a block takes the whole width of the article instead, from the `xl` breakpoint. The difference only shows on a screen wide enough for the article to be wider than the column.

## Usage

On a block at the top level of the page, not nested in another one:

```jsx
<div className="full-bleed flex h-40 items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">full-bleed</div>
```

result:

<div className="full-bleed flex h-40 items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">full-bleed</div>

compared to a block without it:

<div className="flex h-40 items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">default</div>

## On a component

Any component that takes a `className`, e.g. a [`Carousel`](/authoring/carousel):

```jsx
<Carousel className="full-bleed">
  <CarouselContent>
    <CarouselItem>
      <div className="flex aspect-[3/1] items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">1</div>
    </CarouselItem>
    <CarouselItem>
      <div className="flex aspect-[3/1] items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">2</div>
    </CarouselItem>
  </CarouselContent>
  <CarouselPrevious />
  <CarouselNext />
</Carousel>
```

result:

<Carousel className="full-bleed">
  <CarouselContent>
    <CarouselItem>
      <div className="flex aspect-[3/1] items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">1</div>
    </CarouselItem>
    <CarouselItem>
      <div className="flex aspect-[3/1] items-center justify-center rounded-2xl bg-surface-container text-3xl font-bold">2</div>
    </CarouselItem>
  </CarouselContent>
  <CarouselPrevious />
  <CarouselNext />
</Carousel>

[`Sandpack`](/authoring/sandpack) is `full-bleed` already.
