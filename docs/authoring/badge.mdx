<Intro>
  Small tags, usually right under the page title.
</Intro>

```md
<Badge href="https://drei.pmnd.rs/?path=/story/abstractions-positionalaudio--positional-audio-scene-st">storybook</Badge>
<Badge href="https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/api/hooks#useloader">suspense</Badge>
<Badge>Dom only</Badge>
```

result:

<Badge href="https://drei.pmnd.rs/?path=/story/abstractions-positionalaudio--positional-audio-scene-st">storybook</Badge>
<Badge href="https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/api/hooks#useloader">suspense</Badge>
<Badge>Dom only</Badge>

A badge is inline, like a link: badges on consecutive lines make one row, and a badge sits in a sentence too, as in "works with <Badge>suspense</Badge>".

---

`color` takes a color role of the theme: `primary`, `secondary`, `tertiary`, `error`, or one of the custom colors `note`, `tip`, `important`, `warning`, `caution`, `storybook` (see `THEME_*` in [configuration](/getting-started/introduction#configuration)):

```md
<Badge color="primary">primary</Badge>
<Badge color="secondary">secondary</Badge>
<Badge color="tertiary">tertiary</Badge>
<Badge color="error">error</Badge>
<Badge color="note">note</Badge>
<Badge color="tip">tip</Badge>
<Badge color="important">important</Badge>
<Badge color="warning">warning</Badge>
<Badge color="caution">caution</Badge>
<Badge color="storybook" logo="storybook">storybook</Badge>
<Badge color="tip" href="https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/api/hooks#useloader">suspense</Badge>
```

result:

<Badge color="primary">primary</Badge>
<Badge color="secondary">secondary</Badge>
<Badge color="tertiary">tertiary</Badge>
<Badge color="error">error</Badge>
<Badge color="note">note</Badge>

<Badge color="warning">warning</Badge>

<Badge color="storybook" logo="storybook">storybook</Badge>
<Badge color="tip" href="https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/api/hooks#useloader">suspense</Badge>

---

`label` adds a de-emphasized label before the text, and `logo` a brand logo, by its [simple-icons](https://simpleicons.org) slug — for now `storybook`, `chromatic`, `discord`, `github` or `npm`; any other shows none:

```md
<Badge href="https://github.com/codespaces/new?template_repository=pmndrs%2Fdrei" label="GitHub" logo="github">Open in Codespaces</Badge>
```

result:

<Badge href="https://github.com/codespaces/new?template_repository=pmndrs%2Fdrei" label="GitHub" logo="github">Open in Codespaces</Badge>

---

A [shields.io](https://shields.io) image is an image like any other: it is not turned into a `Badge`. For a badge that follows the theme, write `<Badge>`.
