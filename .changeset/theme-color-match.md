A fourth theme control, Color match: on, the palette stays true to the seed color (material-theme-builder's `colorMatch`, which takes precedence over the scheme); off, the color is blended into the scheme. The site seeds it with `THEME_COLOR_MATCH` (`true` or `false`, `--theme-color-match` on the CLI), the reader overrides it like the other seeds, outlined and reset on a double-click.
