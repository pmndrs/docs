`Carousel` MDX component: shadcn's Carousel (Embla), with `CarouselContent`, `CarouselItem`, `CarouselPrevious` and `CarouselNext`, documented in the authoring section. Its arrows sit inside the article's width, in a gutter the carousel keeps on each side. In the markdown export of a page, the slides follow one another, without the arrows.
