Add `SPONSOR_CARD` and `LIBS_MENU` (CLI `--sponsor-card`, `--libs-menu`; workflow inputs `sponsor_card`, `libs_menu`): set to `false`, a site that is not a pmndrs library hides the "Sponsor Poimandres" card, and shows its name as a plain link home instead of the menu of the pmndrs libraries. Both stay on by default.
